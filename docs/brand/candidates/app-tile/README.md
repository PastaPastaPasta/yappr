# App-tile candidate

Style: app-tile. A saturated blue tile pairs a white speech bubble with a bold y cutout.
The original tile artwork is retained in revision 2.

The lowercase wordmark spells **yappr** with an unmistakable double-storey a,
two matching p bowls, and one short r terminal. Tight tracking supports the heavy
grotesk character without joining the letters. Baseline: y=48 in the 64-unit viewBox.

Lettering is outlined from Inter (ExtraBold 800 wordmark), optical size 32, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter
Font and license are preserved in preview/reference/. No runtime font is needed.

Colors: #0369a1 primary; #0ea5e9 dark tile; #38bdf8 dark wordmark;
#ffffff and #000000. No additional chromatic accent.
app-icon.svg background: #0369a1.

Weakness: the literal bubble/y tile is familiar rather than highly distinctive.
At favicon sizes the bubble reads before the y. Wordmarks were checked at 40px on white and #121212.
See preview/ for rsvg-convert renders and the labeled inspection sheet.
