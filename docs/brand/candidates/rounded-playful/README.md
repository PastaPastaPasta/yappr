# Rounded playful — revision 2

A wordmark-led identity: chunky, friendly lowercase `yappr`, with closed p bowls,
long p descenders, a round single-storey a, and a soft open r.
The forked y and its hooked descender become the standalone mark.
No dot or bubble container competes with the letter silhouette.

Colors: Yappr blue #0369a1, dark-mode blue #38bdf8, white #ffffff.
No extra accent color. A slim blue edge keeps the white adaptive y visible on white.
App-icon background: #0369a1, full-bleed opaque square.
All letterforms are hand-authored SVG paths; no font or font conversion is used.
Wordmark baseline: y=46 in the 64-unit viewBox; y/p descenders extend to y=62.

Weakness: the y is deliberately simple and relies on its rounded proportions,
rather than a decorative mascot, for distinction. Use the dark variants on dark surfaces.

Renders and the reproducible drawing script live in preview/.
