# Minimal lowercase

A Swiss-minimal, wordmark-led identity: custom medium-weight lowercase yappr.
The small diagonal clip at the upper corner of the tail of the y is the one identifying gesture. Isolated at a
wider proportion, it becomes a quiet typographic mark rather than a mascot.
Matched circular bowls and tight, even pp spacing give the wordmark its rhythm.

All lettering and contours are hand-authored paths. No font was converted;
there is no font dependency or third-party artwork.

Colors: ink-blue #0c4a6e for light surfaces; sky-blue #38bdf8 on dark;
brand blue #0369a1 for the app tile and favicon; white #ffffff for icon subjects
and the dark footer. Dash blue #008de4 appears only in the Dash footer credit.
App-icon background: #0369a1. No additional accent color.

The transparent app foreground is white, intended over the same brand-blue
adaptive background. Its entire subject is inside radius 300; the app tile
uses the same contour at a larger scale, inside the radius 340 safe circle.

Weakness: the isolated y is deliberately restrained and less immediately
chat-related than a bubble or mascot. The medium-weight wordmark is intended
for UI header sizes; use the simplified tiled favicon at 16px.

Preview PNGs cover 16px and 32px marks/favicons, both surface colors,
1024px app tiles and foregrounds, and wordmark/lockup/footer treatments.
