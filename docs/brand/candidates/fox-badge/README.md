# fox-badge — revision 2

A rounded geometric fox yaps out of a deep-blue circular badge.
The open blue mouth notch separates the muzzle and lower jaw;
a restrained white cheek and three rounded yellow ticks add expression.
The lower contour is a deliberate continuous curve, with no neck cut.

Colors: brand blue #0369a1, ink #082f49, orange #ff792e,
white #ffffff, dark-theme sky #38bdf8,
and sound yellow #fdd60f.
Orange preserves the mascot; yellow is the explicitly approved second accent.
App-icon background: #0369a1, opaque and square.

All SVG geometry and lettering are hand-authored; no fonts or font licenses apply.
The sturdy lowercase wordmark is retained. Its baseline is y=46;
rounded bowls overshoot to y=47 and descenders remain inside the 64-unit box.

The app tile and adaptive foreground use independent enlarged scales.
Rendered subject alpha is checked against radii 340 and 300 respectively.
The monochrome mark is one black fox silhouette, including the mouth notch.
The favicon is its own drawing, not the mark scaled down: an orange fox head in
profile, howling, with a peach throat and a dark open mouth, on a transparent
background. It is a vector trace (vtracer, six flat colours) of a raster
illustration the owner chose. Its specks were removed and it was trimmed to fill
the square with a margin of about 1 unit. Two changes keep it legible on light tab bars:
the cream throat is deepened to peach, and a thin rust edge runs along the throat
below the mouth. `favicon-16.svg` is a separate hand-drawn 16 px version, with
one 2-unit square per pixel. It keeps the head, pointed ear, raised snout, peach
throat with its rust edge, and the dark mouth, and drops the fur detail. The
16 px image in `favicon.ico` is drawn from it. Browsers that take the SVG icon
draw `favicon.svg` at whatever size they need.

Weakness: the favicon is an illustration, not a glyph. It is the busiest icon in
a tab strip, and at 16 px it reads as an orange fox head rather than a howl.

The other SVGs were
generated by the original brand workstream's `build.py`, which is not in this repo.
