# Speech-bubble

A softly squared speech balloon and an inset branching y make a confident, compact conversation signature. The custom lowercase yappr wordmark repeats its rounded bowls; open, carefully spaced pp forms keep the name easy to read.

Primary blue: #0369a1. Dark variants: #38bdf8 with #082f49 inset. White reverses the mark on the app tile. No added accent.

App-icon background: #0369a1, opaque and full bleed. The tile subject fits the centered 680px circle; the adaptive foreground fits the centered radius-300 circle.

All lettering is hand-authored geometric SVG paths; no fonts or converted outlines.

Weakness: the speech-balloon category is familiar; ownership comes from the branching y and consistent rounded geometry.

PNG self-checks in preview cover 16px and 32px favicons, marks, wordmarks, lockups, and 1024px icons on white and #121212.
