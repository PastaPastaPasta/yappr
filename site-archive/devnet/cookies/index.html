Cookies & Storage

What Yappr keeps in your browser, and why

Cookies

Yappr itself sets no cookies, and there are no advertising or tracking cookies. Cloudflare, which delivers the site, may set a short-lived security cookie to protect it from abuse.

The website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and builds no profile of you. The Privacy Policy lists every service your browser talks to.

What Yappr stores in your browser

Yappr has no server to remember you, so it keeps what it needs in your browser's own storage. These stored keys, settings, drafts and caches are not sent to Yappr’s authors.

Local storage

Stays on this device, shared by all Yappr tabs, until removed.

Your sign-in key
When you sign in with a private key, Yappr keeps it so you stay signed in. Your identity’s most powerful (CRITICAL) key is the exception (see below).
Kept: until you sign out
Encryption keys
Your encryption key, which opens your messages and private feed, and the keys for the private feeds you follow.
Kept: until you sign out
Session details
Your identity ID, username, profile and balance, so the app can show them straight away.
Kept: until you sign out
Settings, drafts and caches
Theme and other preferences, unsent drafts, your cart, and copies of recently loaded posts and profiles, so pages load faster.
Kept: until you clear site data
Passkeys
Which of your passkeys were added or used in this browser, the names you gave them, and whether Yappr has offered you one. No passkey or key is kept here.
Kept: until you clear site data
Upload service details
If you connect Storacha or Pinata to upload media, the details needed to use your account.
Kept: until you disconnect the service

IndexedDB

Stays on this device until removed.

Wallet sign-in keys
When you sign in with a Dash wallet app, the keys it grants Yappr are stored encrypted, under a device key the browser will not let anyone export.
Kept: until you sign out
Connection ID
A random ID for this browser, so your next wallet sign-in replaces this one instead of adding another.
Kept: until you clear site data

Session storage

Belongs to one tab and is cleared when that tab closes.

Your most powerful key
If the key you enter is your identity’s most powerful (CRITICAL) key, Yappr keeps it only until you close this tab.
Kept: until you close the tab
Tab state
Small markers for this tab, such as a sign-in step in progress.
Kept: until you close the tab

Keeping your keys safe

  • Staying signed in means your key is stored on this device. Anyone who can use this browser profile could use it, so sign out on computers you share.
  • Malicious browser extensions or software on your device could read stored keys. Only install ones you trust.
  • Signing in with a Dash wallet app, where your wallet supports it, gives Yappr limited keys of its own, so your identity's main keys never enter the browser.

Clearing your data

Signing out removes your keys and session from this browser. To remove everything else, use your browser's "Clear site data" for this site. Neither affects anything on Dash Platform.
